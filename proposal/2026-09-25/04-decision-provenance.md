## 4. Current owner's prompt and this proposal's inference

In the originating conversation the owner asked whether recompiling open-source ZCode, editing the `executor.generateText` / `streamText` entry and producing `zcode.cjs` could give Qiven precise model control; then asked whether rewriting the harness would meet Qiven's goal; and finally requested reconsideration of TCA, ADR-0050 onward, implementation architecture and this `qiven-docs` document set. We infer a proposed **every-invocation classification** scope from the owner's `generateText` / `streamText` sketch and the accepted complete-mediation question; this is a reviewable engineering choice, not a verbatim owner mandate. Action mediation remains a separate proposed boundary. The owner later clarified that the active client is **ZCode Windows Desktop rather than standalone CLI**. Those messages are **design direction and authorization to draft and correct a proposal**, not an explicit owner H2 acceptance of a new ADR or a declaration that CA-2 has passed. The implementation sketches in document 02 and the program amendment in document 00 are our engineering inference from this direction and the pinned primary sources. The change from a generic first profile to Windows Desktop is a material correction, recorded here instead of silently changing the evidence target.

The central technical inference has five parts: (a) a version-checked source overlay can carry stronger delivery and action mediation than an unqualified hook path, **if and only if** all claimed call/dispatch paths and the actual loaded build are covered; (b) editing just `executor.generateText` and `executor.streamText` does not automatically authorize every physical provider attempt, because the pinned runners transform messages and retry before their calls to the AI SDK runtime; (c) classifying only the first design call misses later logical invocations even if their physical attempts pass a content check; (d) reusing a bundle selection or prior receipt cannot prove protected input still exists after compaction, a new subagent request, or source/policy/budget/evidence change; (e) the existing asynchronous context and telemetry actor labels cannot, by themselves, authorize a task, phase or Jason role, especially on auxiliary calls without a bound task or a new subagent process. The accepted TCA architecture §12.3 and §17.3 define the full invalidation and cache binding; an adapter's key must not weaken them. These are falsifiable design judgments, not statements contained verbatim in ADR-0050.
